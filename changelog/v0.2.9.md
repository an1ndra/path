# v0.2.9

Response-cache hits are visible on the dashboard.

## Added

- `/admin/stats` now reports `cache.exact_hits` alongside `semantic_hits`, so Path's own
  exact-match response cache can be distinguished from provider prompt caching.
- The dashboard Cache card shows exact hits, semantic hits and cached prompt tokens.

## Changed

- The Cache card labels its percentage as `prompt cache`, making clear that the hit
  ratio is provider prompt-cache usage rather than the response-cache hit count.
